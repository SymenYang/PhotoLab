# PhotoLab 图标

采用内置 image_gen 生成。设计方向：珍珠白圆角底板、半透明照片叠层、靛蓝天空、杏色太阳及青绿色山丘。保留抽象几何轮廓和柔和层次，不使用写实相机。

源文件：AppIcon-source.png。构建脚本使用 sips 生成标准 macOS iconset 尺寸，再用 iconutil 打包 AppIcon.icns；保留源图透明通道。当前为静态图标资源，不是系统可实时重光照的多层 Icon Composer 素材。

生成提示词：

Create a polished macOS app icon for PhotoLab, a personal photo and video library and photographic discovery app. One single finished icon, 1024x1024 square PNG, with true transparent area outside its rounded-square silhouette. Contemporary Apple system app visual language: restrained translucent glass layers, soft bevels and subtle depth, crisp simple abstract shapes, beautifully balanced rounded square; not photorealistic and not a physical camera or lens. An ivory pearl rounded-square foundation containing two slightly offset overlapping photographic frames; the foremost frame is a bold simple abstract landscape with a deep indigo sky, a warm apricot sun disk and a flowing teal hill. Rear frame subtly lavender glass. Central motif large and immediately readable at small Dock sizes. Front-on orthographic, minimal clean geometry, controlled gentle inner reflections, generous but not excessive margins, quiet premium creative tool aesthetic. No lettering, no text, no watermark, no mockup, no desktop, no enclosing presentation background, no extra icon variations, no Apple logo. The image itself is the application icon asset.
