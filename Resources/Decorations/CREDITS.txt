Camera brand logos

Bundled SVG marks identify the camera that captured a photo. These are separate
from PhotoLab's original CC0 stickers. Marks remain the property of their owners;
no affiliation, endorsement or trademark license is implied.

Twelve Commons source pages classify their simple text/geometric logo artwork
as public domain (PD-textlogo); this is a source-reported copyright status, not
a grant of trademark rights. Individual original author/credit statements,
source URLs, retrieval dates, original and packaged checksums, and processing
notes are retained in Brands/*.source.json and manifest.json.

OM SYSTEM: the original white SVG comes directly from the official brand site.
No open-content license is stated. It is a proprietary trademark asset, not CC0
or a Commons PD-textlogo asset. Official terms:
https://www.om-digitalsolutions.com/en/common/terms_of_use_en.html

Transparent sticker variants

Brands/Variants contains 45 SVG assets: original, black and white variants for
13 camera brands, plus Hasselblad's H symbol and Leica's separate script mark.
No rectangular background plates are drawn. Nikon's yellow square/rays are
removed in these sticker variants. Leica's original red circular badge remains
a badge; its black/white versions and separate script mark have no circle.
These are locally prepared display variants, not claims of official brand
color guidelines. The original source artwork remains in Brands/*.svg.

Hasselblad H symbol: vector transcription of the straight-edged polygon from
the official Phocus resource HLogo_Nobackground.png, not an official SVG release.
Source: https://www.hasselblad.com/phocus/
The reference checksum and processing record are in hasselblad-symbol.source.json.
No open-content license is stated; this is a proprietary brand mark, not CC0.
